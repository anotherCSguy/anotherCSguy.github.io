My Story

Hello, my name is Elisha Miller.

I am a game developer from a small town called Kirksville, Missouri. As a young kid I have always loved playing video games and seeing what stories they hold. I remember sitting in my bed or sitting in class drawing game concepts and exploring new ideas of my version of a perfect game. Between fiddling around in Blender and following tutorials for Unreal Engine, my love for games and the process for making them grew. I hope to create fascinating stories and inspire new game developers just like I was.

Elisha Miller 6/20/2026
