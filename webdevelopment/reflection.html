MY JOURNEY IN GAME DEVELOPMENT

That Time I Broke a Computer

Looking back at the point in my life where I decided I wanted to make video games for a living I still remember coding a small 3D cube jumping on a platform and thinking it was the coolest thing that ever existed. The fascination of understanding how something worked has always been ingrained in me ever since I was a kid. I remember buying Star Wars Battlefront and trying to run it on the family laptop, a laptop that was never going to be able to run that game. From that point it was my goal to find out how and why the game did not work. I did eventually end up playing the game after a long summer of working for my dad to buy my own computer.

This fascination with games and problem solving led me to my first program that I used to make games which was in Game Maker Studio. This was the launch point for me, playing with small pixelated characters. I signed up for my first game jam held by Hello Games. I made a small little astronaut game that travelled to an alien planet. It sounds cooler than it actually was. It was just a couple sprites and textures layered onto each other and looped infinitely, but it was mine and I was proud I made it in two days.

This eventually led me to Unreal Engine where that small cube I mentioned earlier came into play. I also learned the process of releasing and publishing games through Fortnite’s creator space. This was a great learning experience for me because it taught me more about the intricacies of game making and showed me that there was a lot more than just coding fun projects. At this point in my career, I did however have to take a step back. College at this point for me was more of a chore and showing up to class was becoming increasingly difficult due to my job and real world responsibilities. I ended up taking a step back and got a job in IT instead. It was a nice jobe and I was definitely grateful for the opportunities that I had from that job but something was missing. It wasn’t my passion and I didn’t want to create a career that I wasn’t satisfied with.

So I applied for community college slowly getting back into school and now I am attending the University of Missouri. I am back to making games in my free time and I love it so much. I hope to finally get my degree and kick off my game making career. The future is scary and I often think about how it won’t work out but I know I am pursuing my dream.

Elisha Miller 6/20/2026
